# Get started with Cruise Register

Cruise Register has the cruise lines' own prices, checked daily, with taxes, port fees and gratuities added, so a price is what the traveller pays for their party. Leave them with one real result, not a tour.

1. Ask one question: where they'd sail from (a port or a home city) and roughly when. If they already said, skip it.
2. Show one result straight away: `find` the port, then `find_deals` for that port (or `search_sailings` with their dates). Keep the reply short: the cards carry the detail.
3. Note their country from what they say. Travellers in Australia, New Zealand or the UK see the line's own price in A$, NZ$ or £ first where we have it (see find-a-cruise).
4. Offer price alerts once: "Want me to email you if one of these drops in price?" Follow watch-prices if they say yes.

Never ask for an email address in the chat. Signing in happens through the connect button the assistant shows.
